Guile
◖
♧
/gaɪl/NounHard

Meaning (English)

Sly or cunning intelligence used to deceive
or trick someone.

हिंदी अर्थ

छल-कपट, चालाकी

Hinglish Explanation

Guile ka matlab hai smartness ka use karke kisi
ko dhokha dena ya apne fayde ke liye chal chalna.

🦊🎭

Synonyms

  • Cunning
  • Deceit
  • Craftiness
  • Trickery
  • Slyness

Antonyms

  • Honesty
  • Sincerity
  • Frankness
  • Straightforwardness

Example Sentence

He used his guile to deceive the guards and escape from the prison.

Memory Trick

Guile → “गाइल”
Ek bahut chalak aadmi jo meetha bolkar peeche se chal chalta hai.

Visual Memory

Ek lomdi (fox) jo mask pehen kar sabko bewakoof bana rahi hai.

Core Idea

Cleverness +
Deception = Guile

Word Family

Guileful (adjective) – full of guile
Guileless (adjective) – innocent, without deceit

Common Collocations

use guile · guile and deceit · act of guile

MCQ

SSC Practice
